What we do

Crafting a web presence for businesses whose character deserves showing.

You already know the thing that makes your business worth choosing. It is usually the first thing a website loses: too particular for a template, and the part you would have mentioned straight away if someone asked you in person.

Mare Leborgne is a boutique web design studio in Sydney, building sites the way a good jacket is made. Measured first. Cut by hand. Finished to last.

The approach

Focus on what you know best. We will do the rest.

Before anything is drawn, we spend time on the business itself: how it works, who it is for, what it feels like to walk in, and the things nobody thinks to put in a brief. Everything that follows comes from there.

The aim is a site that leaves someone with the impression they would have had standing in front of the real thing. Typography, pace, colour and wording are chosen for one business only, so that arriving on the page feels like arriving somewhere rather than reading about it.

You will not be handed a page builder or asked to choose a layout. We design it, show it to you, and keep working until it is right. Deciding how it should look is the part you are handing over, and it is the part we do best.

Nothing is bought in and reskinned, and there is no template underneath. From the first conversation to the finished site in your own name, it is handled end to end.

Our Mission

To design with style, in a world full of repetition.

A business with real character deserves more than a page that lists what it does. It deserves somewhere a visitor can feel what the place is like and why it is the way it is, and want to stay a moment longer than they meant to.

Templates and generated pages are everywhere now, and they arrive at the same few answers. Style is not decoration against that. It is the thing that says a person made this, for this, and for no one else.

We expect it everywhere else: buildings worth walking past, objects worth keeping, a room you are happy to sit in. A website is where most people meet a business first, and it is the one place we still accept the generic.
