What we do

Crafting web presence for businesses whose character deserves showing.

It is very common for a business to have a unique character that sets it apart, be it in its products, services, values, or the way it interacts with customers. A huge investment is often made in creating this character, only to see it lost in a generic website template. A website is the digital showroom and it has the power to create a lasting impression.

Mare Leborgne is a boutique web design studio in Sydney, crafting sites the way a good jacket is made. We do not just build a website for your business, we create a digital presence that showcases your brand in a stylish way.

The approach

Focus on what you know best. We take care of the rest.

We carry out the design process end to end. We study the business itself: how it works, what its character is, what are its values, and what it sells.

Then we design a site that gives your visitor the same feeling as walking into your store or testing your products, services or experiences.

You will not be handed a page builder or asked to pick a layout. We design it, show it to you, and refine it until it is right. You focus on running your business and we take care of the rest.

Everything is crafted for your business, custom-made.
